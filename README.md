# Case Studies

This repository presents a curated collection of product case studies by [Joise MP](https://joisemp.github.io).

The studies document real-world digital products, from the problem and user context to the workflows, decisions, outcomes, and future opportunities. They serve as a focused portfolio of product design, development, and operations work.

## Featured work

- [BusNest](./busNest.html) — A connected transportation platform for managing school fleet operations across registration, seat allocation, routes, drivers, and maintenance.
- [Services](./services.html) — A facility maintenance workflow that replaces fragmented requests and paper records with assigned tickets, task tracking, verification, and operational evidence.
